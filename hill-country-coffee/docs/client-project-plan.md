Hill Country Coffee is a cafe located in Central Texas. Hill Country Coffee wants to be a place where people feel comfortable and think it's handmade and not corporate. # 3. Target Users & User Needs

## Maya — Mobile Regular

**Context:**  
[Briefly summarize relevant context.]
Maya is a regluar at the cafe. She uses her smartphone to look up the cafe. Only scans the text. Look on the website for a particular thing.

**Primary goal:**  
Here primary goal is to find infomation quickly.

**Important information/tasks:**  
confirm opening hours; quickly find the nearest cafe information; check drink or food option is available;

**Likely friction:**  
When infomation is hard to see when on a small screen. when info is to vage.

**Design/accessibility implication:**  
Fast scanning, touch-friendly controls,readable text

## Daniel — Hill Country Visitor

**Context:**  
Daniel is visiting Central Texas and want to vist a cafe.

**Primary goal:** 
understand what kind of place Hill Country Coffee is; 

**Important information/tasks:**  
Uses photographs and concise descriptive copy to evaluate unfamiliar places.

**Likely friction:**  
a site assumes he already knows the business;  important information appears only inside images.

**Design/accessibility implication:** 
Content should make sense regardless of entry page. important information must be available as real text rather than embedded only in graphics. 

## Renee — Deliberate Planner

**Context:**  
Renee likes meeting friends at local cafes and likes to check her options.

**Primary goal:**  
review menu categories;
share useful information with a friend;

**Important information/tasks:**  
May zoom text or use increased browser text sizing. Reads headings and supporting details.

**Likely friction:**  
text is small or low contrast; - menus depend on tiny PDF documents or images;

**Design/accessibility implication:**  
Readable contrast, flexible layouts, meaningful headings, descriptive links, and text that remains usable when enlarged are particularly important.
